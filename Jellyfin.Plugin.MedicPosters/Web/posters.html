Animated posters for your whole library are not recommended on Fire TV sticks, smart TVs and other low-end devices. They have a small processor and little memory, and a screen full of animated posters can make them slow, stutter or crash. Animated library covers are fine, as there are only a few of them. Try a few posters with Use this one on your slowest device before making them all.

Settings

Libraries

Untick a library to leave it alone, such as an IPTV one. Libraries added later are ticked.

Posters

Jellyfin sends GIF posters at their full size (that's what keeps them moving), so every poster on a page is downloaded whole. Smaller, shorter and fewer colours make lighter files.

Library covers

Your own text and moments

Give a library's cover or a title's poster its own text, and pick the films and moments it's cut from. A library of streams, such as IPTV, can take its cover from another library's films and still show its own name, or any text you like.
